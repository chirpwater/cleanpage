# Privacy statement

Clean Page runs entirely on your device. Once the page has loaded, it makes no network requests of any kind — nothing you type is ever sent anywhere.

Your writing is kept only in local drafts stored in your browser, alongside your chosen settings for letters and text size. Clean Page uses no cookies, no IndexedDB, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

For offline use, your browser caches a copy of the application's own files — its pages, styles, scripts, and fonts. That offline copy never includes anything you have written.

Printing and saving happen entirely on your device, through your browser's own print and download tools. Clean Page has no server, so there is nothing to log, share, or lose.
