# Privacy Policy

Clean Page is published by ChirpWater, LLC ("ChirpWater", "we", "us"). This policy explains what we do with your information. The short answer is that we never receive any of it.

Effective September 19, 2026.

## We do not collect your information

We do not collect, receive, store, or otherwise obtain any information about you, and we do not collect anything you write in Clean Page. This is not a promise to handle your information carefully. There is no information for us to handle.

## We do not collect analytics

We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, or measurement of any kind. We do not count visits, record usage, or build profiles. We do not know who uses Clean Page, how often it is used, or what anyone writes in it.

## Everything stays on your own device

Everything you write in Clean Page is processed entirely on the device you are using. Writing is not saved automatically. Closing or reloading the tab clears the page; save or download your writing to keep a copy. Only your font, size, and color choices are saved in browser storage. Your writing is never sent to us, and it is never sent to anyone else.

## We do not monetize your information

ChirpWater does not sell, rent, share, license, or otherwise monetize your information, and we do not permit anyone else to do so. We could not do any of these things even if we wished to, because we never collect your information in the first place.

## We do not disclose your information

We do not disclose your information to any third party, and that includes law enforcement and other government authorities. Were we ever compelled to produce your information, we would have nothing to produce.

## Children

We do not knowingly collect information from children, for the same reason we do not collect information from anyone else: we collect none at all, whatever the age of the person writing.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published here with a new effective date. We will not begin collecting your information without first saying so plainly on this page.

## Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to ChirpWater, LLC at [chirpwater.com](https://chirpwater.com).
